## Trending / Breaking Search Tips
- **Counting Day Metadata**: Add `<meta name="news_keywords" content="DMK, AIADMK, BJP, NTK, [Winning Candidate Name], [Key Constituency]">`.
- **Dynamic Titles**: Update the title tag via JS or server-side injection during leads: `DMK leads 150 seats | TN Election Results 2026 LIVE`.
- **Indexing Speed**: Use a `lastmod` with hourly timestamps in `sitemap.xml` and ping Google Search Console manually every hour on counting day.

## Google Discover & News Optimization
- **High-Res Imagery**: Ensure the homepage hero image is at least 1200px wide and has a descriptive alt tag.
- **Performance**: Maintain a Core Web Vitals score above 90.
- **Publisher Transparency**: Ensure the JSON-LD includes clear "creator" and "publisher" data.

## On-Page Placement Guide
- **H1**: Tamil Nadu Assembly Election 2026 — Live Results
- **H2s**: Party Wise Seat Count, Constituency Results, Vote Share Analysis
- **Alt Text**: "Tamil Nadu election 2026 seat tally chart", "Constituency wise winner map TN 2026"
- **Clean URLs**: Use `/constituency/[slug]` and `/party/[slug]` format.
