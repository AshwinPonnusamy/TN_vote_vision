# Social Sharing Strategy: TN Vote Vision

## Viral Distribution Channels

### WhatsApp (Primary in TN)
- **Direct Share Button**: Implement a button that triggers:
  `whatsapp://send?text=🗳️%20Tamil%20Nadu%20Election%202026%20LIVE%20results%20→%20https://tnvotevision.netlify.app`
- **Group Sharing**: Encourage users to share constituency-specific lead updates to local community groups.

### Twitter / X (Journalists & Analysts)
- **Automatic Hashtags**: Include #TNElection2026, #TamilNaduElection, #TNVoteVision in sharing links.
- **Live Lead Screenshots**: Add a "Download Image" button for charts to facilitate manual sharing with high visual impact.

## Rich Preview Optimization
- **Dynamic OG Images**: For constituency pages, generate dynamic OG images showing:
  - Constituency Name
  - Leading Candidate
  - Vote Margin
- **Large Image Cards**: Use `twitter:card` set to `summary_large_image` to dominate the feed.

## Peak Engagement Timing
- **08:00 AM (Counting Day)**: Initial leads and trends.
- **11:00 AM (Counting Day)**: Major swings and party-wise dominance.
- **04:00 PM (Counting Day)**: Final victory declarations.

## User Engagement Hooks
- "Is your constituency orange or red? Check the live map now!"
- "The closest race in TN right now: [Constituency Name]. Follow live."
